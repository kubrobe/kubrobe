## What it is

Doran is a Korean course for Android, aimed at the language an exchange student actually needs. It runs entirely on the phone, with no account and no internet connection after install.

## Built so far

- **26 units** on a lesson path: six for Hangul, then Basics, Everyday and Exchange life (campus, dorm, part-time work, transport, health, polite speech).
- **12 exercise types**, from learning new letters and matching pairs to building sentences from a word bank in both directions, filling blanks and speaking a sentence aloud.
- **Bundled Korean audio** for every word and sentence, at normal and slow speed. It is generated by a script, and each clip is checked by the speech recogniser.
- **Offline pronunciation scoring.** Your voice is recorded and checked on the phone by a Korean speech recognition model (sherpa-onnx), and you see which syllables were heard well.
- **Learning loop.** Hearts, XP, a daily goal, a streak, a placement test, practice sessions and a word list.

## How it is built

The course content is written in one plain-text file and compiled to JSON by a Python script. Lesson generation, answer checking and scoring live in a plain Kotlin `core` package that is unit tested. Unit tests generate every lesson of every unit with 40 random seeds and check that each one can be answered. Device tests run on Android 14 and Android 8 emulators in CI, play complete lessons through the real interface, and make 2,000 random taps to check for crashes.

## Status

In active development. The first commit was on 19 Nov 2026, and the interface was rebuilt in phases on 22 Nov 2026. The pronunciation score has only been checked against the bundled audio in CI. The emulator has no real microphone, so scoring a real voice still needs testing on phones.
